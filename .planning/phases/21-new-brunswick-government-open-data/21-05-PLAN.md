<objective>
Crown land and forestry is completed here, and the parcels-and-civic-address domain — GeoNB's
largest group (23 services) and its strongest geocoding surface — gets its two highest-value tools.

These are the two biggest layers in the whole portal: 604,520 parcels and 373,172 civic address
points. Together they are the reason threat T-21-03 exists. Both tools refuse an unfiltered call
before a single byte leaves the process, following BC's `bc_get_water_wells` precedent — the
alternative is an agent's context window destroyed by a well-formed request.

Crown Land itself already shipped as the Wave 0 tracer. This plan adds the remaining two DNR tools —
mineral occurrences and provincial parks — **when the Wave 0 manifest still contains them**. The
21-01 checkpoint on the gnb.socrata.com discovery surface may have swapped both to the long tail to
hold the tool budget at 22; if so they remain reachable through `nb_query_geonb_layer` and this plan
ships only the parcels pair. Read `constants.ALL_NB_TOOL_NAMES` — it is authoritative.

The mineral-occurrences service is the sharpest illustration of the layer-id discipline in this
phase: its layers are published out of order (0, 1, 7, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 6), so positional reasoning
about "the first layer" is wrong twice over.

Purpose: ship NB's geocoding surface safely and close out Crown land and forestry.
Output: 2 to 4 curated tools with pre-network guards on both large layers.
