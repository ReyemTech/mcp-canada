<objective>
The last two curated domains and the transport surface.

Health and education are the phase's two **dispatch** tools: GeoNB publishes each health facility
type as its own layer inside one service (hospitals under Horizon, hospitals under Vitalité,
after-hours clinics, adult residential facilities, nursing homes, pharmacies — layers 0 through 5),
and public schools as anglophone and francophone layers. One tool with a validated type argument is
worth six near-identical tools, and it is what D-07's curation bar asks for.

Transport is the divergence CONTEXT.md chose deliberately. Saskatchewan and Nova Scotia deferred
511 entirely; New Brunswick ships **stubs** (D-09) so the capability is discoverable and ready the
moment a key exists. `511.gnb.ca/api/v2` was probed across seven endpoint paths at plan time and
every one returned HTTP 400 with an invalid-key error body — the service exists and is gated, it is
not absent. The Manitoba `Five11NotConfigured` pattern maps onto it exactly.

The critical constraint on those three tools (D-10): an unconfigured key is a **normal outcome**,
not an exception path. The tool returns a structured envelope, the integration test asserts its
exact shape, and the project-wide catch-all coverage gate still applies in full.

Purpose: complete the curated surface and ship transport in a discoverable, honest state.
Output: 5 tools — 2 dispatch, 3 gated.

**Validation note:** the spec-less probe fallback was skipped for this phase (no SPEC.md). Truths
derive from the CONTEXT.md `<domain>` boundary, D-07/D-09/D-10 and 21-RESEARCH.md.
