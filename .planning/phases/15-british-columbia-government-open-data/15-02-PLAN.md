<objective>
Implement the 5 BC Data Catalogue (CKAN) discovery tools and the two-step routing workflow that glues CKAN dataset discovery to WFS feature queries. This plan fills in the CKAN half of client.py and adds exactly 5 standalone @tool functions to tools.py. The curated WFS tools (Plan 03) depend on `bc_query_features` routing logic but do NOT touch client.py stubs written here — Plan 03 implements its own `_wfs_fetch` body.

Output: Populated client.py CKAN functions + 5 @tool functions in tools.py + fully filled-in TestFetch* and TestBc* test classes for those 5 tools.
